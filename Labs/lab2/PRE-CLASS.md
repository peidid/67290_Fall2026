## Why it has to be now and not on the day

Tableau will not let you save anything until you have clicked a link in an email it sends you. If that email does
not arrive on Saturday afternoon, you lose the lab.

Three things go wrong, and all three are fixable on a Monday and not on a Saturday:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The email goes to Junk.** | University mail filters catch it more often than you would expect. Check spam before you decide it never arrived. |
| **The link expires.** | Tableau gives you a nominal 48 hours. If you miss it, use *Forgot Password* to trigger a new one — the account is not lost. |
| **Campus wifi.** | Twenty-five sign-ups from one network can get throttled. **Sign up from home**, not from Education City. If it fails, Tableau's own advice is to retry from a different device on a different network. |

**Sign in once on the laptop you are bringing Saturday.** Not your phone — see below.

## One thing to know before Saturday

Tableau Public is the **public internet**. In the browser there is no "save to my computer" — pressing Save
publishes your work to a public profile under your name.

We will set your profile to **hidden** in the first five minutes of the lab, which keeps your work out of search
results and off your public profile page. We will also explain exactly what hidden does and does not protect, so
you can make your own call.

The data itself is Qatari government open data — nothing sensitive. What becomes public is your name attached to
your work.
